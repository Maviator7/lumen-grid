# Four-direction city growth

Approved user scope: extend the existing continuous city with east/west growth and meaningful terrain differences visible before opening. Keep north/south growth, city identity, wiring, demand rules, and existing saves. Do not add batteries or change the 45-second gate in this increment.

Offer four directions in stable order North, South, East, West. Preserve the old north/south generation and bool APIs. North/south are riverbank regions with existing river crossing constraints. East/west add dense and detour regions, with seed-dependent, deterministic restricted foundations that really reject crossing cables. Both lateral terrain kinds must be visible among the two lateral options. Show direction, terrain, demand/deadline, added cable/material/dedicated/bridge budgets, supply facilities, and a small plan preview in a 2x2 modal fitting a 1280x720 viewport.

Lateral growth attaches to frontier relays on the requested outer side, adds two feeders, a district and optional supply facilities. Dense terrain obstructs the direct data-side relay to demand connection; a short choke-point route exists. Detour terrain obstructs the same direct shortcut and supplies a longer route around the restricted area. Players can choose other legal routes. Extend beyond all existing site and obstacle bounds to avoid placing a new restriction across an existing wire. Validate full future reference wiring and actual service quality in a planning clone before offering an available choice. Never modify the live city while previewing. Unavailable candidates retain a visible reason.

New sector direction, terrain, local demand-route edges and obstacle rectangles are added to version-1 save snapshots. Missing direction means infer north/south from the original North bool. Missing obstacles/routes mean empty. Original generator v1 and RulesVersion remain unchanged. Capture/restore must deep-copy mutable geometry, reject invalid geometry/directions, and reject saved links crossing the restored restrictions. Old north/south sectors are never regenerated. Terrain is simulation-owned and shared by render, planning/Connect, reference solver, and save validation.
