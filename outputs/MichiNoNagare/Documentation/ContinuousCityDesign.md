# Continuous expanding city

Main play uses one continuously running city. The fixed tutorial and seed-generator reference validation retain the 45-second round. Main play starts running, permits live cable connect/repaint/remove/undo, and never enters Result at 45 seconds. Preserve simulation time, delivered counters and existing wiring during edits and expansion. Re-route in-flight packets from their departure node (also when already travelling) after topology changes, keeping original birth/deadline; impossible routes fail explicitly. Cache routes per topology and support multiple power/data sources, with district lists derived by kind rather than fixed indexes.

Expansion unlock: meet electricity >=98%, recent data success >=90%, mean latency <=2.8s and delivery to every active district for 45 consecutive simulation seconds. Display quality from a rolling 15-second window; a bad interval resets the stability timer. The player chooses when to expand. Opening expansion/map/reset/tutorial dialogs temporarily freezes simulation without changing the user's pause flag.

Offer north/south sectors with seeded district type and demand. Add two relays and one disconnected destination, with two utility facilities every other expansion or whenever reference validation requires them. Automatically extend same-bank feeder corridors from existing relays, routing around obstacles; the player connects the cross-river route, destination and utility facilities. Existing city continues to operate. Grant actual feeder length plus 36 cable units, two dedicated slots and one bridge per sector. The initial source gains modest output per sector, and local utility facilities support larger cities. Keep root seed/terrain and district positions stable. Build additional ground/canal as bounds grow. Focus new area after opening, support pan/zoom across the growing bounds and add whole-city framing.

Reset confirmation restores the seed's original sector and wiring. Seed generation creates a new root city; ordinary pause/resume does not reset records. Main F2 remains a reference network helper for all open sectors, while F1 retains confirmation. Japanese labels and inspectors must handle arbitrary node IDs and growing budgets without clipping. No main-game terminal victory modal.

Verification: time beyond45 remains live; actual stable unlock; failed unlock cannot mutate city; expansion preserves old edges/time/counters, adds demand/resources, invalidates stability; repeated north/south expansion has a solvable reference network; live edits/undo/rerouting remain safe; multiple sources and unique edge IDs; reset removes expanded sectors; legacy/tutorial/seed regressions; native expansion-choice and continuous operation UI.

Expansion offers are validated atomically in a planning clone with a legal reference network and up to 120 seconds of actual simulation. Invalid offers remain unavailable; the live city is never partially mutated. Local reactors connect on the demand bank, while data cores feed the opposite bank, preserving the need to build the crossing.
