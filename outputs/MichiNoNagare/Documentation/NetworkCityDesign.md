# LUMEN GRID — first network city
Approved direction: futuristic night city supplied with electricity and data. Power is continuous flow; data is discrete packets with latency. Move away from car imagery and dashboard cards.

## One-level loop
Fixed power source, data source, five relays and three city districts. Connect or repaint links, then run a 45-second repeatable test. Limited cable length and three dedicated links. Shared relay and cable capacity is consumed first by continuous power; the remaining switching capacity serves packet queues. This is an explicit fictional shared-infrastructure rule, not a physical electricity model.

Power allocation is max-min fair along shortest permitted paths, constrained by source, cable and relay capacities. Data packets move with propagation delay, queue for capacity and expire at a deadline. A district must be powered to use delivered packets. Initial topology concentrates both flows through the centre. Solution adds a southern power trunk and forbids power on the old approach, releasing the centre for data. Both demands must be met; sacrificing industry is not a win.

## Visuals
Deep midnight navy, amber continuous power, cyan discrete data, thin emissions, high-rise night skyline, lit windows reflecting district service. Full-screen city; unboxed telemetry and contextual node inspector. Node/link selection rather than driving roads. No vehicles.

## Scope
One map, nodes fixed, connect/edit/erase/undo, capacity inspection, route restrictions, deterministic round and a native Mac build. Legacy Riverside remains as reference. No physics-grid power engineering, city growth, audio, free placement of towers or multiplayer.
