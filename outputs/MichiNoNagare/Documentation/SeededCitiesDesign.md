# Seeded cities v1

Keep the fixed tutorial; add a seed picker and independent generated cities. The native app opens a generated city, with tutorial replay available from H. G opens an explicit reset/generation dialog with a signed 32-bit seed. The seed and generator version are visible. Regenerate a seed to restore its initial layout; preserve a layout when redesigning after a test. A completed tutorial can open generation directly.

A stable integer PRNG drives source/relay positions, the order of three destination districts, demand, river position, restricted rectangles, skyline and district architecture. Keep ten logical roles for this first generator, while varying actual positions and connection lengths. All baseline/solution edges must respect distance, obstacles, cable and bridge budgets. Generate a known solution, check it in the actual 45-second simulation, and accept the deterministic candidate only when it clears; otherwise advance deterministic candidates, with bounded retries and a known safe fallback. Keep visible constraints accurate: restricted blocks disallow conduit segments; river crossings consume a bridge slot. Remove/undo refund bridge counts. Dedicated limit remains three, cable budget varies with known solution plus slack. This is a first family of independent maps, not an endless growing world or arbitrary graphs.

Buildings: industrial low factories with stacks/service tanks, residential towers with varied wings and heights, medical campus with a luminous cross and helipad. Reserve skyline space away from gameplay nodes and constraints. Fixed Japanese role labels remain; tooltips show district demand. Seeded visuals regenerate identically for a seed. Destroy old scene roots/materials on regeneration.

UI: retain auto-measured text; show seed/version, cable budget, bridge budget. Dialog buttons explicitly reset current wiring. Disable map replacement while a simulation runs. Modal typing must not trigger tool shortcuts, launch or world edits. H returns to a fresh fixed lesson after explicit reset; ordinary F1/F2/F3 operate on the current city. Blank and known solution views remain development helpers and recovery controls.

Validation: determinism across extreme seeds and repeat generation; varied layouts/demand/obstacles; many seeds all clear with the reference solution; initial graphs comply with all constraints; blocked segments, bridge budget/refund, undo and reset; old tutorial and network regressions; native seed dialog, generation and visual QA including readable budget labels.
